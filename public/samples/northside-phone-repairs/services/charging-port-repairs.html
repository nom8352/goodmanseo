What this page covers

Charging faults can be caused by lint, damaged cables, board faults or the port itself. This page keeps the promise practical: inspect first, clean where appropriate, and quote the right repair.

Typical repair path

  • Cable, charger and port inspection
  • Careful cleaning where debris is the cause
  • Port replacement quote if the connector is damaged
  • Charging, data connection and microphone-area checks where relevant
