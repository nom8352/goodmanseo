# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

GoodmanSEO serves Korean-speaking small business owners in Australia, especially Sydney and nearby regions. They are busy owner-operators who need their business to look trustworthy online without learning every detail of marketing, SEO, or web operations.

Their practical job is to understand how customers see the business across Google Business Profile, website, Instagram, and Facebook, then decide what is missing and what to fix first.

## Product Purpose

GoodmanSEO reviews a local business's online presence across Google, website, Instagram, and Facebook, then helps the owner improve inquiry flow through the right next step: free check, paid diagnosis, website work, SEO setup, Google Business Profile support, social setup, or AI operations mentoring.

Success means the owner can quickly answer:

- How does my business look online right now?
- What looks incomplete or inconsistent to customers?
- What should I fix first?
- What service fits my current stage?

## Brand Personality

Friendly, practical, trustworthy.

The voice should feel like a calm partner helping a small business owner make sense of their online presence. It should not sound like a pushy agency pitch, abstract strategy consulting, or a generic website builder.

The emotional goal is confidence and relief: the owner should feel, "now I know where to start."

## Anti-references

Avoid:

- generic website-agency landing pages
- abstract "online structure" consulting language
- salesy social ads or hype-heavy marketing copy
- long explanatory sections that make busy owners work too hard
- near-duplicate social-card design options that only change surface styling
- treating price as the lead message before problem, fit, and next action are clear

## Design Principles

- Lead with the customer-visible outcome: how the business appears online, what is missing, and what to fix first.
- Keep one idea per section or line whenever possible.
- Use short, human Korean sentences that a busy owner can scan quickly.
- Preserve the Google + website + Instagram + Facebook "seen together" story across every page and social asset.
- Keep pricing visible and easy to compare, but secondary to the problem, fit, and next action.
- Make visual routes structurally distinct, not superficial variants.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No project-specific accessibility requirement is recorded. Use WCAG AA as the baseline.

Prioritize:

- readable Korean copy on mobile and desktop
- sufficient text contrast on the dark visual system
- keyboard-accessible forms and navigation
- visible focus states
- reduced-motion alternatives for animation-heavy sections
- simple language for non-technical users
