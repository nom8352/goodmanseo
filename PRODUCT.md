# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

GoodmanSEO serves Korean-speaking owners of local service businesses in Brisbane and nearby suburbs (North Lakes, Moreton Bay, Sunnybank). Their customers are mostly English-speaking locals who find a business by searching "service + suburb" on Google. The owners are busy and do not want to learn SEO; they want the phone to ring from search.

Priority industries: beauty (lashes, nails, skin, brows, hair), trades (air conditioning, electrical, plumbing, refrigeration), and cleaning (bond, carpet, commercial). Also a fit: mechanics, massage and physio, tutoring.

## Product Purpose

GoodmanSEO gets a local business to show up on Google Search and Maps for the searches its customers type, then keeps it there. The work is five things done completely and consistently: choosing the customer searches, completing the Google Business Profile, keeping reviews coming, aligning the website copy to the searches (building a one-page site if there is none), and reporting impressions, clicks and rankings every month.

There are only three public products: 무료 순위 점검, 구글 상위 노출 3개월 프로그램, 월 유지 관리. Website production is offered only when a business needs separate service pages.

Success means the owner can quickly answer:

- Where does my business rank right now for the searches customers type?
- Why is it not showing, and what gets fixed first?
- What happens in each of the three months, and what did the numbers do?
- Do I keep going, and what does keeping it cost?

## Proof

The owner ranked their own North Brisbane photography studio with the same method (three months: 131 clicks, 4,450 impressions, daily impressions from 0 to about 170; main keyword #3 organic and #1–2 on Maps when checked in November 2026). On the site this case is anonymised: no business name, URL, address, or keyword list; the Search Console chart is the visual. It is described as "직접 관리하는 가게", never as a client.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, practical, trustworthy. Plain-spoken about what SEO can and cannot do.

The voice is a fellow business owner who did this for their own shop and now does it for others, not an agency. It never promises guaranteed rankings; it shows dated numbers and reports every month.

The emotional goal: "이 사람은 진짜 올려봤고, 뭘 하는지 다 보여주네."

## Anti-references

Avoid:

- website-agency landing pages that lead with design, packages and sample galleries
- "basic setup" or "cleanup" language that makes the service sound like an add-on
- "상위노출 보장" claims or any guaranteed-outcome wording
- AI-education, AI-report, or AI-news content mixed into the main site
- KakaoTalk buttons and personal photos (owner decision)
- long explanatory sections that make busy owners work too hard
- treating price as the lead message before the outcome, proof, and next action are clear

## Design Principles

- Lead with the outcome and the proof: the Search Console chart and dated numbers come before any feature list.
- One idea per section; short, human Korean sentences a busy owner can scan on a phone.
- Show the method as five numbered steps so the service reads as SEO practice, not web design.
- Keep the three programs side by side with prices visible, the middle one highlighted.
- Keep the navy / off-white design system from `DESIGN.md`; the 2026-11 reset changed content and offers, not the visual language.
- Every CTA says 무료 순위 점검; the form asks for three customer searches so the promise and the entry point match.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No project-specific accessibility requirement is recorded. Use WCAG AA as the baseline.

Prioritize:

- readable Korean copy on mobile and desktop
- sufficient text contrast on the dark navy bands
- keyboard-accessible forms and navigation
- visible focus states
- reduced-motion alternatives for animation-heavy sections
- simple language for non-technical users
