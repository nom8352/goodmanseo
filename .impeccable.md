## Design Context

### Users
Korean-speaking small business owners in Australia, especially in Sydney and nearby regions. They are usually busy, not very technical, and want a website or social profile that makes their business look trustworthy and easy to contact.

### Brand Personality
Friendly, practical, trustworthy. The tone should feel like a calm partner helping a business get online, not a pushy sales pitch. The emotional goal is confidence and relief.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clear, concise, and approachable. Copy should be short, direct, and easy to scan on desktop and mobile. Avoid overly promotional language, heavy jargon, and long explanations. Keep the same tone across the website, Facebook, Instagram, and Threads.

### Design Principles
- Lead with the benefit or outcome first, not the price.
- Keep one idea per line or section whenever possible.
- Use short, human Korean sentences.
- Keep pricing visible, but secondary to the message.
- Make every word earn its place.
