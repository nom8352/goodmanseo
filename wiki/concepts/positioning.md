# Positioning

Last updated: 2026-06-16

## Core Position

GoodmanSEO is not mainly a website builder. It is a practical online-channel review and cleanup service for local service businesses.

The lead question is:

> How does my business look online right now?

## Audience

Primary:

- Sydney and Australia-based Korean local service businesses

Secondary:

- Remote clients who need Korean and English support

## Channel Story

GoodmanSEO reviews these together:

- Google Business Profile
- Website
- Instagram
- Facebook

The key customer problem is not "I need a prettier site." The stronger problem is:

- customers see fragmented information
- trust signals are missing
- inquiry flow is unclear
- the owner does not know what to fix first

## Preferred Language

Use:

- online channels
- review
- free check
- what is missing
- what to fix first
- inquiry flow
- Google, website, Instagram, Facebook together

Avoid leading with:

- online structure
- vague consulting language
- generic website agency language
- abstract strategy claims before concrete checks

## Tone

Write like a practical small-business advisor:

- clear
- direct
- owner-friendly
- not hype-heavy
- not too technical
- not decorative for its own sake

## Messaging Test

Before changing public copy, ask:

1. Would a busy local business owner understand it immediately?
2. Does it explain what gets checked or improved?
3. Does it preserve the free-check entry point?
4. Does it connect Google, website, Instagram, and Facebook as one customer-viewed system?
