# GoodmanSEO SEO Audit - 2026-06-25

Audit date: 2026-06-25  
Site: `https://goodmanseo.com/`  
Scope: full site technical SEO, on-page SEO, content structure, and priority action plan  
Skill used: `seo-audit`

## Executive Summary

Overall health: **Good foundation, several high-impact cleanup opportunities**

The site is crawlable, HTTPS-only, has a live sitemap, robots.txt reference, static HTML pages, canonical tags, and 23 expected URLs responding with `200`.

The highest-impact SEO opportunities are now mostly about:

1. **On-page metadata depth**: all 14 blog meta descriptions are short.
2. **Structured data quality**: static pages currently carry generic `ProfessionalService` schema; blog pages should expose article schema in static HTML.
3. **Performance SEO**: blog images are large PNG files, many above 1.3 MB.
4. **Internal linking and topical structure**: blog posts should link back to service pages and related posts.
5. **Local SEO trust signals**: footer social links are placeholders and schema `sameAs` is not useful yet.

## On-Page SEO Findings

### 5. Blog meta descriptions are too short

Impact: High  
Priority: High

Evidence:

- 14 of 23 generated pages have meta descriptions under 70 Korean characters.
- All 14 short descriptions are blog detail pages.
- Shortest observed descriptions:
  - `mismatched-info-across-channels`: 36 chars
  - `before-starting-seo`: 42 chars
  - `why-website-does-not-convert`: 45 chars

Why it matters:

- Short descriptions waste SERP snippet space.
- They often do not include enough query context, location context, or conversion framing.

Fix:

- Expand each blog `excerpt` in `src/data/blogPosts.js` to roughly 80-120 Korean characters.
- Include:
  - the problem
  - the business context
  - the practical takeaway

Example direction:

Current:

```txt
온라인 채널마다 정보가 다르면 고객은 생각보다 빨리 불안해집니다.
```

Better:

```txt
구글, 홈페이지, SNS의 연락처와 서비스 정보가 다르면 고객 신뢰가 떨어집니다. 작은 비즈니스가 먼저 맞춰야 할 정보 정합성을 정리합니다.
```

### 6. Some page titles are generic or not mapped to search intent tightly enough

Impact: Medium  
Priority: Medium

Evidence:

- Base pages have valid unique titles, but some are broad:
  - `홈 | Goodman SEO`
  - `블로그 | Goodman SEO`
  - `서비스 및 가격 | Goodman SEO`

Why it matters:

- The site wants to rank for local service intent such as:
  - Korean business website in Australia/Sydney
  - Google Business Profile setup/cleanup
  - online presence audit
  - SNS/homepage/Google channel review

Fix:

- Map primary keyword intent per page.
- Consider more descriptive titles:
  - Home: `호주 한인 비즈니스 온라인 점검 | Goodman SEO`
  - Pricing: `홈페이지 제작 · 구글 비즈니스 · SEO 가격 | Goodman SEO`
  - Blog: `호주 한인 비즈니스 온라인 운영 블로그 | Goodman SEO`

### 7. Footer social links are placeholders

Impact: Medium  
Priority: Medium

Evidence:

- Footer social links use `href="#"` for Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube.
- `src/data/siteSeo.js` has `sameAs: [siteUrl]`, which does not provide external entity confirmation.

Why it matters:

- Placeholder links are weak trust signals.
- Real social profiles can support entity confidence and E-E-A-T.

Fix:

- Replace `#` with real GoodmanSEO social URLs or remove the icons.
- Update schema `sameAs` with real profiles:
  - Instagram
  - Facebook
  - YouTube
  - LinkedIn if available

### 8. Blog detail pages need stronger internal links

## Structured Data Findings

### 12. Static schema is generic across all pages

Impact: Medium  
Priority: Medium

Evidence:

- 23 generated static pages contain JSON-LD.
- Sample static outputs all expose `@type: ProfessionalService`:
  - `dist/index.html`
  - `dist/pricing/index.html`
  - `dist/blog/ai-website-design-before-prompting/index.html`

Why it matters:

- Blog detail pages should expose `Article` or `BlogPosting` schema in the static HTML.
- Service pages can benefit from `Service`, `FAQPage`, or route-specific structured data where appropriate.
- Relying on client-side injected JSON-LD may be less reliable than static generated schema.

Fix:

- Update `scripts/generate-static-pages.mjs` to generate route-specific JSON-LD:
  - Home: `ProfessionalService` + `WebSite`
  - Blog detail: `BlogPosting`
  - Pricing/service pages: `Service`
  - FAQ sections: `FAQPage` where visible FAQ content exists

## Performance Findings

### 13. Blog images are heavy PNG files

Impact: High  
Priority: High

Evidence:

Largest image files:

- `public/blog/manage-without-outsourcing-everything.png`: 1.76 MB
- `public/blog/what-is-included-in-free-audit.png`: 1.74 MB
- `public/blog/before-building-new-website.png`: 1.66 MB
- `public/blog/why-ai-web-design-feels-generic.png`: 1.64 MB
- `public/blog/why-empty-google-business-profile-hurts.png`: 1.62 MB

Why it matters:

- Large PNGs can hurt LCP, bandwidth, and mobile performance.
- Blog listing loads many images, even with lazy loading.

Fix:

- Convert blog images to WebP or AVIF.
- Generate responsive sizes.
- Use `srcSet` / `sizes` for blog cards and detail hero images.
- Keep original PNGs only if needed as source assets, not delivery assets.

### 14. Main JS bundle is over the warning threshold

Impact: Medium  
Priority: Medium

Evidence:

- Build warning:
  - JS bundle around 504 KB minified
  - Vite warns chunks are larger than 500 KB

Why it matters:

- Larger JavaScript can slow mobile rendering and interaction.

Fix:

- Code-split route pages using dynamic imports.
- Consider lazy-loading Three.js-related dependencies if they are not needed on first load.
- Keep YouTube iframe lazy-loaded, already done.

### 15. Cache and security headers can improve

Impact: Low to Medium  
Priority: Low

Evidence:

- HTML cache: `public, must-revalidate, max-age=0`
- JS asset cache: `public, must-revalidate, max-age=14400`
- HSTS missing

Fix:

- For hashed assets, use longer cache:
  - `public, max-age=31536000, immutable`
- Add HSTS if safe.
